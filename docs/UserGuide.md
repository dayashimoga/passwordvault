# User Guide & Operations Manual

## 1. Getting Started
1. **First-Time Setup:**
   - Launch the application on your computer or mobile device.
   - Choose **Create New Vault**.
   - Enter a strong Master Passphrase (we recommend a 4- to 6-word passphrase generated using the built-in generator).
   - Carefully record your **Cryptographic Emergency Recovery Key** on paper and store it in a secure location (e.g. fireproof safe).
2. **Biometric Convenience Unlock:**
   - On supported devices (Windows Hello, Touch ID, Face ID, Android Fingerprint), you may enable Biometric Unlock in Settings.
   - Biometric unlock provides rapid convenience access while your device remains trusted. If your device restarts or biometric settings are altered, your Master Passphrase will be required.

---

## 2. Managing Your Credentials
- **Add Items:** Click `+ New Item` or press `Ctrl+N` / `Cmd+N`. Choose between Login, Note, Card, or Identity.
- **Generate Passwords:** Open the Password Generator to customize length, character sets, or passphrase word count.
- **Search:** Instant search filters across titles, domains, and tags as you type (`Ctrl+F` / `Cmd+F`).
- **Autofill:** Use browser extensions or mobile autofill services to fill logins seamlessly without copying passwords to the clipboard.

---

## 3. Backups & Disaster Recovery
- Go to `Settings -> Backup & Export`.
- Create a timestamped, encrypted backup (`.vaultbak`).
- In case of a lost or broken device, your vault can be fully restored on any new device using your Master Passphrase or your Emergency Recovery Key.
